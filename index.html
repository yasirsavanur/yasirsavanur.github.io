The useful question is rarely just what the number is.

My first planning role taught me to look for the point where the plan and reality had come apart. Sometimes the answer was in the ERP. Sometimes it was in the way people were using it. That habit has followed me through manufacturing, software validation, environmental reporting and national education data.
